# Mark Mods Required or Optional
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

Required and optional mods let a collection stay flexible while keeping its core intact. When you reinstall the collection later, you can choose whether to install the optional mods.

## Required mods

Required mods are essential for the collection to work. Players cannot skip them during installation. They are typically framework mods such as SMAPI and Content Patcher, and other mods that the collection depends on. 

## Optional mods

Optional mods enhance the experience and are not strictly necessary. Players can skip them during installation. They include visual enhancements and alternative configurations.

## Instructions

On the collection's edit page in the Workshop, open the **Mods** tab. In the **Instructions** panel, click **Add**.

Instructions appear during installation.

- For a required mod, the instruction is a hint in the bottom corner.
- For an optional mod, the instruction pauses installation and offers a skip option. Use this when a player should be able to skip that mod.
