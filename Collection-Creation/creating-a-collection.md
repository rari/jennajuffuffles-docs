# Creating a Collection
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

{% hint style="danger" %}
This guide is for creating your own original collections, not for copying or repackaging existing collections. Collections include curated configurations and sometimes custom patches that belong to the original curator or others.
{% endhint %}

## Create a collection in the Workshop

Go to **Collections** > **Workshop** > **Create a collection**, then choose **From Profile**, **Empty**, or **Quick Collection**.

You can upload your collection to Nexus at any time to save a draft. Drafts are private and can only be deleted until they are published.

### Create from Profile

Choose **From Profile**. Name the collection, then click **Create**.

It makes a collection from the mods enabled on your current profile. A collection from a profile includes mods that are enabled on the current profile, 

A collection created from a profile does not include mods that were disabled.

Before you choose **From Profile**, disable any collections active there:

1. In Vortex, go to the **Mods** tab.
2. Filter for the active collection name or names on your current profile.
3. Disable the collection. When prompted, click to include recommended mods.

### Create an Empty Collection

Choose **Empty**. Name the collection, then click **Create**.

An empty collection starts with no mods. Use it when you are building a collection from scratch.

An empty collection includes only the mods you add to it.

On the Mods page, right-click a mod and choose **Add to Collection**. To add several at once, select them with Ctrl, Shift, or Ctrl+A, then use the menu bar at the bottom.

### Quick Collection

Choose **Quick Collection**. Read what is included, then click **Proceed**.

A Quick Collection is a backup of your current mod list, for another PC or mod manager. It takes a few clicks and does not include every feature of a full collection.

A Quick Collection includes:

- Nexus mods that are currently enabled and deployed.
- Installer choices for mods that support installers, such as FOMODs.
- File conflict rules.
- Load order rules.

A Quick Collection does not include:

- Mods from sources other than Nexus Mods.
- Changes you made to mods after installing them.
- Output from automated tools generated on your PC.
- Mods you created on your PC and added to Vortex.

If nothing is included, the Nexus mods you want in the collection need to be enabled and deployed.

## Outside Sources
For mods that did not come from Nexus, you will need to provide additional information. Double-click on that mod once in the Mods panel to edit its source information. You can add Mod Name, Author, Version, Category, Source and Section. 

warning hint: do not add mod type unless you are doing something very specific here. this should be left blank for 99% of mods.
