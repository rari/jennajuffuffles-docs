# Update with Amethyst
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

{% hint style="danger" %}
Update the collection, not individual mods. Collections are tested together. Updating individual mods outside a collection revision can cause unexpected behavior.
{% endhint %}

To update the collection revision in Amethyst, follow [Updating a collection](https://github.com/ChrisDKN/Amethyst-Mod-Manager/wiki/3.4-Installing-a-collection#updating-a-collection) in the Amethyst wiki.

A new revision does not require downloading the whole collection again. Amethyst lists the mods it will remove, add, or update, then downloads the new and changed mods, removes the ones the revision dropped, and applies the collection's load order again. Mods you installed yourself stay in place.
