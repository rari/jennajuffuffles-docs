# VERY Expanded Collection Documentation
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

This site documents the Nexus collections curated by JennaJuffuffles. They are fun to play with friends, and built for experienced players to enjoy on their own too. 

## Need help?

* [Discord](https://discord.gg/MPcgJUXeeY) for support and updates
* [Nexus Mods status](https://nexusmods.statuspage.io/) when downloads or the site fail
* [Check your SMAPI Log](Troubleshooting/your-smapi-log.md) to see (and share) what your SMAPI is doing
