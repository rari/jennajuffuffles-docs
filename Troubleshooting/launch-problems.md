# Launch Problems
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

## The game does not start

### What you see

Stardew Valley never reaches the title screen, or it closes as soon as you launch it.

### What to do

1. Restart the computer, then verify the game files in your store launcher.
2. Launch once through the same path you configured for SMAPI. On Steam Deck, launch **Stardew Valley from Steam**. Do not double-click `StardewModdingAPI.exe`.
3. Read [Your SMAPI Log](your-smapi-log.md).

### If it still happens

- Vortex: confirm SMAPI is the primary tool, then use [Install with Vortex](../Install/vortex.md) and [Vortex Notifications and Solutions](vortex-notifications.md).
- Steam Deck: use the runtime section of [Steam Deck](../Install/steam-deck.md). Linux SMAPI with a Proton game, or the reverse, will not start.
- Everyone: check [Known Issues](known-issues.md).

## SMAPI opens and no mods load

### What you see

The SMAPI console appears, and the mod list is empty or almost empty.

### What to do

1. Confirm you launched the game, not a second copy of the executable.
2. Read [Your SMAPI Log](your-smapi-log.md) for "failed to load" or an empty mods folder.
3. Vortex users: an empty Vortex folder message is explained on [Vortex Notifications and Solutions](vortex-notifications.md).

### What should happen

SMAPI lists the collection's mods, and red errors are either absent or already covered on [Known Issues](known-issues.md).

## The game crashes while loading

### What you see

Loading is slow, including when you start a new game, and the game crashes before you can play.

### What to do

Stay in the game window while it loads. Alt-tabbing or clicking away can cause the crash.
