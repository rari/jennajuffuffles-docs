# Reset Your Content Files
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

Verify Stardew Valley in your store launcher, then launch the game once so the store can finish setup.

## Verifying Game Files

Before installing mods, make sure your game files are healthy and up to date.

### How to Verify

**Steam:**

1. Open Steam Library.
2. Right-click **Stardew Valley**.
3. Select **Properties > Installed Files**.
4. Click **Verify integrity of game files**.

**GOG:**

1. Open GOG Galaxy.
2. Right-click **Stardew Valley**.
3. Select **Manage Installation > Verify / Repair**.

**Xbox / Game Pass:**

1. Open the Xbox app.
2. Right-click **Stardew Valley**.
3. Select **Manage > Files > Verify and Repair**.

### After Verifying

1. **Run the game once** through your normal launcher (Steam, GOG, or the Xbox app).
2. Let it complete the initial setup.
3. Close the game.
