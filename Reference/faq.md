# FAQ
*Applies to: Stardew Valley 1.6.15+ Last reviewed: 2026-10-02*

## Collections and Nexus

### Is Nexus Premium required?

No. Nexus Premium is not required.

### Where are these collections available?

These collections are only available on Nexus.

### Why Nexus Collections?

Nexus Collections is used because it gives the collection creator the tools for a curated experience, and because Nexus has active moderation and pays mod creators. These were day zero priorities.

### When should mods be updated?

For best results, update mods only when the related collection updates.

### What should I do if I crash while loading?

If loading is slow, including on a new game, avoid alt-tabbing or clicking away. That can cause a crash.

### Can I get a zip of all files?

No. Please do not ask how to pirate mods included in these collections.

### Can I clone bundled content in public collections?

No. Redistribution of bundled content is not permitted. Some files are original to these collections, and others belong to third-party creators and are included under their terms. Personal use is fine.
